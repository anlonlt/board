## How you work (the same for every part; written by the kit, not the orchestrator)
1. **Context, not a summary:** `python3 -I .kit/kit.py train <TRAIN> --zoom <PART>` shows the train so far: what each earlier step found, its `check` and `handoff`. Read the owner's words above yourself (never trust a paraphrase of them, this prompt's included); a number from an earlier step is rechecked with its `check` command before you build on it.
2. **Turn 1:** `python3 -I .kit/board_cli.py update posts <POST_ID> --data '{"session_id":"<SESSION>","status":"in progress","body":"<one line: what you are doing first>"}'`. Keep your own working checklist with TaskCreate/TaskUpdate if it helps you; it stays yours.
3. **Your result is the post, in the kit's format** (`.kit/BOARD.md` § The post format): `verdict` (one plain sentence answering the task), `bars` (each number with its denominator), `artifacts` (repo paths: they open in the board's viewers), `not_checked`, `check` (the one command that reproduces your main number), `breaks` (how to make that check fail: a command that plants a fault or feeds a bad input, or `manual:` and what the review does, for a browser or Microsoft 365 step), `handoff` (what the next step needs), and the `body`: the whole result, after a first line saying what the owner must know or do. Say it once, on the board: the post is complete, messages only point to it; the lead says what he must know, the rest under `##` sections, which fold as he likes (`.kit/BOARD.md` § Sections fold, `kit.py sections`); a repo document (`docs/<PART>.md`) only for a report to keep or what a post body cannot show (pictures inline, diagrams), embedded as `` `docs/<PART>.md § Section` `` and never restated. Pick the board feature for each kind of content from `.kit/BOARD.md` § Which feature for what (audio takes, pictures to compare, a question for the owner).
4. **Done:** `python3 -I .kit/kit.py done <POST_ID>` must say OK (it checks the format and that your artifacts exist, runs your `check`, which must pass, then applies `breaks` in a throwaway copy of your work and reruns `check`, which must fail: a check that cannot fail is refused); commit and push your work; `python3 -I .kit/end_session.py`; then `SendMessage` to `{{SLUG}}-orchestrator`: `Done. Check post <POST_ID>.` (if the send fails, the orchestrator is mid hand-over: wait 30 s and send again). A review of your step starts by itself.
5. **Then stay available:** the board clerk forwards the owner's comments on your post to you, each with the one command that answers it in his thread (`board_cli.py reply`). Talk to him in {{TALK}}, plainly, on the board, never at length in chat; a turn the clerk or a session opened ends with `·` (the owner typing in your chat gets a short answer in words).
6. **Heavy commands through the device:** downloads, installs, imports/conversions, training, full test suites and builds run through the device, e.g. `python3 -I .kit/device.py run -- pip install -r requirements.txt` (add `--kind fetch|install|import|train|tests|build` when the command does not say it). It waits until the job fits in this device's memory, runs it, and learns what it took, so heavy jobs never crash the computer and run side by side only where they fit. A light command (ls, git, a quick script) runs directly.
